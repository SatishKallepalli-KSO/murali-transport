# Get muralitransport.com on Google

Your site can appear in Google search and on your Google Business listing. Do both steps below.

## 1) Google Business Profile (fastest for Dommeru search)

Your search link is for the local business listing. Add the website there:

1. Open [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/) and sign in as the owner of **Murali Office Miny Lorry Transport**.
2. Choose the Dommeru listing.
3. **Edit profile** → **Contact** / **Website**.
4. Set website to: `https://muralitransport.com`
5. Save.

Google often shows this website link on the right-side business panel within a few hours to a few days.

## 2) Google Search Console (indexes the whole site)

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add property → **URL prefix** → `https://muralitransport.com`
3. Verify ownership (DNS TXT in Cloudflare, or HTML file upload).
4. **Sitemaps** → submit: `https://muralitransport.com/sitemap.xml`
5. **URL inspection** → enter `https://muralitransport.com/` → **Request indexing**.

Already on the live site for crawlers:

- `https://muralitransport.com/robots.txt`
- `https://muralitransport.com/sitemap.xml`
- LocalBusiness structured data in the homepage

Indexing is not instant — expect days to a couple of weeks for new sites.
