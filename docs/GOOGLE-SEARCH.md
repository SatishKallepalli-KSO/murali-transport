# Get muralitransport.com on Google

You do **not** need an existing Business Profile. Create one, then attach the website.

## A) Create Google Business Profile (Dommeru listing)

1. Open **[business.google.com/create](https://business.google.com/create)** (sign in with a Google account you control).
2. **Business name:** `Murali Office Miny Lorry Transport`  
   (same name people already search for).
3. **Category:** choose something like **Transportation service** or **Truck rental service** / **Logistics service**.
4. **Location:** add the Dommeru address  
   `2MFM+F2V, Dommeru, Andhra Pradesh 534342, India`
5. **Service area** (optional): Andhra Pradesh hubs you cover (Rajahmundry, Kovvur, etc.).
6. **Phone:** `9949705008` (and add `8885075008` if the form allows another number).
7. **Website:** `https://muralitransport.com`
8. Finish the flow. Google may ask to **verify** the business (postcard, phone, email, or video). Complete verification when prompted — the listing becomes fully visible after that.

### After it’s created

1. Open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com/)
2. Select **Murali Office Miny Lorry Transport**
3. Confirm **Website** = `https://muralitransport.com`
4. Add **hours** (e.g. daily 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM), photos (office photo from the About page), and description.

Your Dommeru Google search panel will then show the website link once the profile is live/verified.

## B) Google Search Console (indexes the whole site)

Optional but recommended for appearing in normal web results:

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add property → **URL prefix** → `https://muralitransport.com`
3. Verify (DNS TXT in Cloudflare is easiest, or HTML file)
4. **Sitemaps** → submit `https://muralitransport.com/sitemap.xml`
5. **URL inspection** → `https://muralitransport.com/` → **Request indexing**

Already on the live site for crawlers:

- `https://muralitransport.com/robots.txt`
- `https://muralitransport.com/sitemap.xml`
- LocalBusiness structured data on the homepage

Indexing can take days to a couple of weeks for a new site/profile.
